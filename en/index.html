FAQ

Questions about dining

Short answers on portions, booking and pricing.

How big are the portions?

A portion is about 150 g of kebab, served with pide, grilled vegetables and salad (fictional demo information).

Do I need to book?

Recommended for weekends and larger groups; book by form or WhatsApp (demo).

Are the images and data on the site real?

No; this is a setup example. Images are representative and names, figures and people are fictional.

How are prices determined?

Menu prices follow current meat prices; this demo does not include a price list.
